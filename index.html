💔 For the Broken Heart

When a person left, someone died, or life broke your expectations.

Emergency Rules for Today:

  • 📵
    Rule 1: Put the phone on Airplane Mode for 30 minutes Do not text them. No begging. No rereading old chat screenshots. Protect your peace.
  • 💧
    Rule 2: Drink one glass of cold water Crying rapidly dehydrates your body, giving you a headache and fatigue.
  • 🛌
    Rule 3: Wrap yourself in heavy blanket warmth Heartbreak activates the same neural pain centers as physical burns. Warmth physically calms the vagus nerve.

💸 For the Empty Wallet

When bills are due, savings are dry, and money stress is choking you.

Zero-Peso Dopamine Menu (100% Free Comfort):

  • 🚿
    The Long Scalding/Cold Shower A free sensory reset. Cleanses cortisol and gives your brain a fresh baseline.
  • 🚶
    The Aimless Neighborhood Stride Leave your wallet at home. Walk for 25 minutes. Movement signals your brain you are not trapped.
  • ☕
    Hot Water with a Pinch of Salt or Calamansi Settles stomach acid caused by hunger, stress, and anxiety.

Financial Reality Triage:

When you are broke, you cannot think in 5-year plans. You only think in 24-hour survival:

1. Food & Water first. Everything else can wait.
2. Debt collector shame is not deadly. You are still worthy as a human being even if your bank account is at ₱0.00.
3. Money comes and goes in seasons. You will not be in this exact spot forever.

🩹 For the Sick & Aching Body

When illness, migraine, or physical exhaustion takes over.

Low-Battery Mode (Only do what is necessary):

  • ⏸️
    Cancel the guilt of doing nothing Healing is an active biological job. When you sleep, your white blood cells and immune system are working hard.
  • 🫁
    Drop your shoulders & unstick your tongue Notice right now: is your tongue pressed against the roof of your mouth? Drop it. Unclench your jaw.
  • 🍵
    Warm fluid sip by sip Even warm tap water or broth helps blood vessels relax and reduces pain sensitivity.
